# Wheelwright

**A companion for your digital footprint.**

Everything you make with AI leaves something behind: projects, sites, scripts, ideas in progress, conversations. Wheelwright gives all of it a center. It grows around what you care about: keeping your world in order, watching over your work, welcoming the tools you already use, and getting better the longer you use it.

At the center is **Otto**, your guide to Wheelwright and to getting the most out of it. Like a good concierge, Otto quickly learns what you look after, what you hope to achieve, and where it can help right away. Each project you take on later gets a helper of its own, **Ozi**, working under Otto's eye.

## One prompt, not a product

This repository contains no app, no package and no server. It is a prompt: a short set of plain-language files that your own AI assistant reads and follows. That changes what you get.

- **It grows into your shape.** A product ships the same features to everyone. Wheelwright starts small and builds only what your goals call for, one wave at a time, so two people end up with two different wheels.
- **You can read every rule.** Nothing is hidden in code. Its principles, its loop and its safeguards are sentences you can read, question and change.
- **It works with the AI you choose.** Any capable assistant that can read files and run commands can follow it: Claude Code, Codex, pi, ruflo, or whatever comes next. When models get better, your wheel gets better, with nothing to upgrade.
- **It improves itself, with your say-so.** Each wave ends by asking what went well and what did not. The wheel proposes changes to its own rules, and you decide.
- **Nothing counts until it is confirmed.** Work is checked by someone other than whoever did it, and every decision is written down in your own words.
- **It stays yours.** What it learns lives in your folders, not on someone else's servers. MIT licensed.

## Start

1. Make a new, empty folder for your wheel.
2. Open your AI assistant there.
3. Tell it: **"Read START.md in github.com/wheelwright-ai/otto and wake Otto up."**

Otto starts by getting to know you, and asks before writing anything outside its scratch folder.

Your conversations can keep their value too: [Tracks](https://github.com/wheelwright-ai/tracks) turns AI conversations into portable records you own.

## What is inside

- CHARTER.md: principles and governance
- START.md: where Otto begins, the order of authority and the loop
- SMOKE.md: the living check of what must always hold
- GOALS.md, WORK.md, BOARD.md: your goals; the work (lugs and initiatives), its proof, saving and record; the board
- WAVES.md: the waves and what seals each one (earlier records call a wave a chamber)
- archive/v0.1/: the earlier version, kept for reference
- journal/: how this prompt was built (history, not instructions)
