# Wheelwright

Wheelwright grows your AI-work setup the way a nautilus grows its shell: one chamber at a time, each sealed behind it once proven, all on one spiral driven by your goals. Mission: recursive self-improvement, in a transparent and collaborative way.

Every cut (one pass of the loop) starts from your current goals and smoke-tests what must already be there. It plans each piece so a small model can build it, has someone other than the builder verify it, smoke-tests again, and repairs anything that went missing before it seals.

This repository is prose only: seed v1, chamber 0. Read CHARTER.md and START.md before your agent does.

## Start
1. Make a new, empty folder for your wheel (never inside this repository).
2. Open your coding agent there (Claude Code, or any agent that reads files and runs commands).
3. Copy this repository somewhere it can read, and tell your agent: "Read START.md in the seed and grow my wheel."

Your agent will interview you about your goals first and write nothing outside its scratch folder until you say yes.

- CHARTER.md: principles and governance
- START.md: order of authority and the cut loop
- SMOKE.md: the living smoke test
- GOALS.md, WORK.md, BOARD.md: the interview and goals, work items and the ledger, the board and its design craft
- CHAMBERS.md: the chambers and their seal conditions
- archive/v0.1/: the earlier seed, sealed reference
- journal/: how this seed was built (not instructions)
